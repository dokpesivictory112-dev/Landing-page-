Why prices in the market are higher

Every hand an item passes through adds a markup

By the time an item reaches a shop in Nigeria, it has usually passed through an importer, a wholesaler, and a retailer. Each one adds their own margin on top of the factory price. You end up paying for all of it.

  • No travelling to China yourself to buy direct
  • No needing to speak Mandarin to negotiate
  • No middlemen adding a markup you never see
